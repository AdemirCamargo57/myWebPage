<div class="page-heading"><span class="eyebrow">State University of Goiás</span><h1>Theoretical and Structural Chemistry Group of Anápolis</h1><p class="deck">Quantum chemistry and molecular simulation applied to the structure, properties, and reactivity of chemical systems.</p></div>

<article class="prose page-body" markdown="1">

The group brings together work in theoretical and structural chemistry at the State University of Goiás in Anápolis, Brazil. Its research uses electronic structure calculations, molecular dynamics, and statistical analysis to understand organic and inorganic compounds, bioactive molecules, and materials.

## Research areas

- **Molecular electronic structure:** quantum chemical descriptions of bonding, electron density, and molecular properties.
- **Solvation and molecular interactions:** ab initio simulations of hydrogen bonding, ion complexation, and environmental effects.
- **Molecular reactivity and photochemistry:** reaction mechanisms, excited states, and nonadiabatic dynamics.
- **Computational tools:** development of software for molecular analysis and preparation of simulation inputs.
