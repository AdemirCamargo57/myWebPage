Physical & Computational Chemistry

Understanding molecules.
Sharing knowledge.

I am Ademir Camargo, a professor of physical chemistry at the State University of Goiás in Anápolis, Brazil.

My work combines quantum chemistry, electronic structure calculations, and molecular dynamics to understand molecular properties, interactions, and reactivity. This website brings together my research, scientific publications, and teaching materials.

Molecular structure and dynamics

From electronic structure to the motion of atoms, I study how molecular interactions shape the behavior of chemical systems.

  • Ab initio molecular dynamics and solvation
  • Excited-state dynamics and photochemistry
